How Gemma 4 Powers URFeast

URFeast combines open-weight AI vision and reasoning with verified human community expertise and deterministic safety guards.

The 7 Core Roles of Gemma 4 in URFeast

1. Perception

Reads meal photos and packaged food ingredient labels into structured observations with evidence tags.

2. Instant First Response

Provides immediate personalized verdict and breakdown before peer or dietitian replies arrive.

3. Personalization

Evaluates every meal or label specifically against the user's saved Diet Passport (allergies, diet type, goals).

4. Safety Audit of Human Advice

Audits peer and dietitian replies synchronously, flagging allergen or diet-style conflicts with explicit warning chips.

5. Triage for Dietitian Review

Detects medical/clinical keywords and routes complex posts into the clinical queue for verified dietitian review.

6. Synthesis of Final Plan

Synthesizes 3-5 concise, actionable steps from the thread while strictly filtering out any unsafe audited suggestions.

7. English-Only Output

Generates all analysis summaries, safety warnings, and synthesized plans in clear English for consistent user experience.

Open Models & Deterministic Architecture

Gemma 4
Core Multimodal & Reasoning Engine: Configured via GEMMA_MODEL env var via Gemini API, with local fallback via Ollama (GEMMA_BACKEND=ollama).
faster-whisper
Speech-to-Text: Open-source Whisper small model running int8 quantization on CPU with VAD filter for fast voice query transcription.
Piper & gTTS
Text-to-Speech: Open-source TTS configured for English-only voice output for consistent, user-friendly playback.
Python Math & Rules
Deterministic Guards: Non-AI serving math (sugar teaspoons, sodium to salt conversions, package totals) and synonym allergen matcher.

Honest Limitations & Safety Disclaimer

  • Not Medical Advice: URFeast provides educational food information and label breakdown. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace medical care.
  • No Absolute Allergen Guarantees: Image analysis cannot detect microscopic cross-contamination. Always verify physical package labels and ask manufacturers directly.
  • Deterministic Math Safety: Nutrition math is computed deterministically by Python to prevent LLM arithmetic hallucinations.
